Privacy Policy (Demo)

How information submitted through this website would be handled in a real implementation.

Demo notice: This is a prototype privacy policy. The wording below is placeholder content and is not legal advice. All privacy, data-handling, and legal content must be reviewed and finalized — ideally with qualified legal counsel — before this site is used in production.

1. Information we collect

Through the forms on this site, we may collect:

  • Parent or guardian contact information (name, phone, email)
  • Child name and age, for inquiry purposes
  • Program interest
  • Tour preferences (dates and times)
  • Messages you submit through our forms

2. How we use information

  • Responding to inquiries
  • Scheduling tours
  • Enrollment communication
  • Internal administrative follow-up

3. Child information

Only minimal child information (such as first name and age) should be collected through public website forms. Detailed and sensitive information — including medical records, allergy details, immunization records, custody arrangements, and emergency contacts — should be collected through secure, official enrollment paperwork, not through a public demo form on this website.

Please do not submit sensitive medical or custody information through the public forms on this site. We will request that information through a secure, appropriate process during enrollment.
