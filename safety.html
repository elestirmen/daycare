Health Policy

Sick child policy (summary)

To protect every child in our care, children who are ill may need to stay home until they are well enough to participate comfortably and are no longer contagious. This typically applies to symptoms such as fever, vomiting, or a contagious illness.

  • Parents are notified promptly if a child becomes ill during the day.
  • Clear return-to-care guidance helps keep everyone healthy.
  • Specific timeframes and requirements follow the center's official policy.

This is a demo-safe summary. Please contact the center for the current, complete sick child and health policy before relying on any specific detail.
